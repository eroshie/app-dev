# My Favorite Movie: American Sniper

> A war drama about courage, duty, and the cost of service.

## About the Movie

**American Sniper** is a 2014 American biographical war film directed by *Clint Eastwood*. It is based on the autobiography of **Chris Kyle**, a U.S. Navy SEAL sniper who served during the Iraq War. Bradley Cooper plays Kyle.

---

## Why I Like It

1. Strong acting, especially from the lead role
2. It shows both the battlefield and life back home
3. It makes me think about the sacrifices of soldiers and their families
